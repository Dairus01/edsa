## Scope and visitor mode

The complete EDSA homepage: the approved restoration hero with immediate
CTAs, a compact service-information rail, then concise identity,
experience, biblical teaching, generations, visit, human invitation,
and utility footer scenes. Mode: make practical action immediate, then
persuade through a short, visually varied ministry story.

## Audience, job, and proof

A mobile-heavy first-time visitor needs to understand EDSA without
decoding photography, find service times and directions immediately,
and feel welcome without unsupported promises. Organisation content,
structured Sapele location data, confirmed social links, and real EDSA
photographs are the proof. Names, leadership titles, denomination,
amenities, service duration, and guaranteed outcomes remain out of
scope.

## Direction and memorable moment

Preserve the approved navy-black hero, then change scene deliberately:
paper utility rail, asymmetric ivory manifesto, midnight photographic
experience, focused preaching stage, amber generations field, clean
visit utility, and a human full-bleed invitation. Manrope handles the
post-hero interface while Newsreader gives Scripture and selected
declarations an editorial voice. The preaching depth handoff remains the
only substantial post-hero desktop interaction.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

The hero remains exactly 2.25 viewport screens on desktop and 1.75 on
mobile. Its rendering, sequence loading, frame mapping, reduced-motion
logic, and scroll physics remain unchanged. Essential meaning stays in
normal text flow; mobile, coarse-pointer, and reduced-motion experiences
use compact static compositions. No homepage photograph is reused.
Sunday 8:00 PM is displayed exactly as supplied and remains flagged for
church confirmation before launch. No deployment is part of this work.
