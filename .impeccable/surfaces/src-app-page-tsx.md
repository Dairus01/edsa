## Scope and visitor mode

Phase 2A of the homepage only: hero release, Who We Are, What We
Believe, and a temporary boundary. Mode: Persuade through
understanding, without adding the visit-planning action reserved for
Phase 2B.

## Audience, job, and proof

A first-time visitor needs to understand what EDSA is, what the
community believes, and why it may matter to them. The supplied
explanatory copy and existing real church photographs are the proof;
unsupported doctrine, leadership details, history, and promises remain
out of scope.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the approved hero’s navy-black, brand-surface, Outfit, and warm
light language into an asymmetric editorial narrative. The memorable
moment is the move from symbol to people: the first worship photograph
emerges from the hero release, then changes from restrained monochrome
to full human color as the visitor reads who EDSA is.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

Phase 1 hero implementation is immutable. Motion remains quieter than
the hero and fully optional. Phase 2A ends before visit content,
service details, and the footer. The supplied Sunday time of 8:00 PM
still requires church confirmation before public launch.
