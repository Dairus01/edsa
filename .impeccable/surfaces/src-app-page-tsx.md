## Scope and visitor mode

The complete EDSA homepage through Phase 2B: the approved restoration
hero and Phase 2A story, followed by lived church experience, biblical
teaching, intergenerational belonging, first-visit reassurance,
practical visit information, a human invitation, and footer. Mode:
persuade through understanding, then make the visit action immediate.

## Audience, job, and proof

A mobile-heavy first-time visitor needs to understand EDSA without
decoding photography, find service times and directions quickly, and
feel welcome without unsupported promises. Supplied church copy,
confirmed location and social links, and real EDSA photographs are the
proof. Names, leadership titles, denomination, amenities, service
duration, and guaranteed outcomes remain out of scope.

## Direction and memorable moment

Preserve the approved navy-black hero and carry its warm, human energy
into a light editorial document using Outfit, deep navy type, generous
space, and asymmetric real-photography compositions. The memorable
Phase 2B moment is a restrained depth handoff between people teaching
Scripture, ending with a young voice; motion then declines toward an
almost-static visit block and a warm, people-led final invitation.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

The hero remains exactly 2.25 viewport screens on desktop and 1.75 on
mobile, with no post-hero opaque logo or restored brand plate. Essential
meaning remains in normal text flow; mobile, coarse-pointer, and
reduced-motion experiences use static teaching imagery. Sunday 8:00 PM
is displayed exactly as supplied and remains flagged in central content
for church confirmation before launch. No deployment or Phase 3 launch
polish is part of this phase.
